# Hawta · Bons plans du Maroc

Design reference: [awesome-design-md](https://github.com/voltagent/awesome-design-md), particularly its [Airbnb marketplace analysis](https://github.com/voltagent/awesome-design-md/blob/main/design-md/airbnb/DESIGN.md).
Use its marketplace clarity as inspiration, adapted to our own identity.

Interaction and accessibility reference: [Vercel Labs Web Interface Guidelines](https://github.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines).
Apply keyboard access, visible focus, managed dialog focus, meaningful labels, loading/error feedback, reduced motion, browser zoom support and responsive safe-area checks. Use semantic links for navigation and preserve filters in URLs where appropriate.

- Use a cool off-white canvas, ink-navy text, cobalt actions, lime highlights, teal for measured proof and burgundy for advertised promotions.
- Use Plus Jakarta Sans for the full interface. Strong, compact headlines and tabular prices form the hierarchy.
- Let products and prices lead. Keep introductory content short on mobile.
- Give each card a clear sequence: image, product, price, evidence, action.
- Separate seller-advertised discounts from measured reductions. Show unknown information honestly.
- Use soft rectangular surfaces, restrained shadows, clear rules and generous spacing.
- Give primary actions visual priority; avoid competing badges and decorative clutter.
- Design for iPhone first: 44px touch targets, 16px form text, safe-area spacing, readable narrow cards and accessible sheets.
- Verify small and large phone widths, navigation, forms and horizontal overflow.
- Reuse existing components and tokens; keep extra dependencies and assets minimal.
