# Marjane Mall discovery and deal-quality review

Inspected 16 October 2026. This is a targeted live browser review, compared against the existing crawler and scoring implementation, not an exhaustive catalogue crawl. No checkout, account access, or production crawl was triggered. No CLAUDE.md/context file was found in the workspace file inventory; findings are compared with docs/CRAWLER-AUDIT.md, not attributed to an unseen Claude conversation.

## Findings and validation list

| Priority | Finding and evidence | Enhancement | Acceptance check |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Per-product prices omit delivery. The live Anker Life U2i page showed 139 DH plus 18 DH delivery after hydration. Official delivery documentation says final charges depend on the selected address; our types/schema have no shipping quote. | Rank known total delivered cost; store quote scope, timestamp and unknown status. Display 139 + 18 = 157 DH only for the observed context, never universally. | A lower sticker price cannot automatically beat a cheaper delivered offer. Unknown shipping is labelled unknown. |
| P1 | Panier Malin advertises 10% extra from 300 DH on an eligible, changing selection, applied automatically at checkout. No conditional basket-promotion model exists. | Capture campaign eligibility, threshold, validity and exclusions separately from base price. Offer a basket planner based on wanted items. | An eligible 300-DH basket gives a conditional 270-DH merchandise estimate; 299 DH does not. Never apply the saving to unrelated products, assume stacking, or call unnecessary extra spending a saving. |
| P1 | Adult relevance is incomplete. A real homepage ADIDAS children's unisex backpack passes fitsPromotionProfile for an adult male profile. | Store age audience separately from gender. Add adult/child/baby and household/gift preferences. | Unisex never overrides an explicit child audience for an adult-only feed. Uncertain audience stays labelled. |
| P1 | Our previous profile patch over-filters accessories: a real laundry bag title mentioning lingerie and sports shoes is rejected as fashion. | Identify the product's main noun/type before reading compatibility or use-case text; separate accessory-for-X from X. | Laundry bags, shoe racks and clothes hangers remain eligible general goods; actual wrong-size garments remain excluded. |
| P1 | Colour is not considered by fitsPromotionProfile. A reproduction accepts the live Adidas white shoe with size 42 for a profile preferring Noir. Parser stores available sizes as a flat string. | Make colour preference explicitly hard or soft, with explanations; retain size × colour × price × stock combinations. | Never badge 'black size 42 available' using black from one variant and 42 from another. This cross-variant failure is a structural risk, not reproduced on this single-colour sample. |
| P1 | The 139-DH Anker/Soundcore Life U2i page links to another Life U2i listing at 199 DH, both labelled MARJANEMALL. Product IDs differ; identity equivalence is not proven. | Compare candidate duplicate listings across aliases (Anker/Soundcore), model, barcode/ref, colour and bundle. | Prove identity before presenting the 60-DH difference as a saving; otherwise show 'possibly the same model'. |
| P2 | New campaign discovery is manual. Homepage links Panier Malin, Trouvailles, weekend flash deals and time-limited campaign modules; configured paths are a fixed list. | Discover allowed campaign links from current navigation/homepage, remove tracking parameters, retain provenance and expiry; crawl a bounded priority queue. | New campaigns are discovered without code edits; stale ones expire; taxonomy, editorial and product links are distinguished. This does not prove their products are absent from other feeds. |
| P2 | Source data itself conflicts: Adidas title says homme while structured Genre says Mixte. Anker title says Anker while brand field says SOUNDCORE; technical attributes include noise-suppression wording that needs interpretation. | Keep field-level source evidence and conflict flags; maintain cautious brand aliases. Show 'source data conflicts' rather than silently treating every spec as reliable. | Conflicting gender/type facts cannot become an unqualified compatibility badge; preserve both observations. |
| P2 | Seller score grants maximum trust to any seller whose name begins with marjane (regex in evaluate.ts). Exact identity is not validated. | Use verified seller IDs or exact allowlisted aliases, keep user preference separate from seller identity. | A synthetic name such as 'Marjane Example Shop' does not inherit official-seller confidence. No live impersonation was observed. |
| P2 | Deal movement compares chiefly with the preceding observation, while public cards also lead with advertised discount. | Add sufficiently sampled 30/90-day typical-price baselines, days observed and confidence; rank value separately from personal relevance. | A hypothetical 100 → 200 → 110 sequence is not promoted as exceptional merely because the last movement is -45%. Sparse history remains explicitly insufficient. |

## Implemented: discovery improvements (17 October 2026)

- Adult/child/all audience controls, corrected household accessory classification and exact fractional shoe sizes. Saved colours now filter fashion by default; the profile checkbox allows relaxing that rule. Ambiguous colour/size combinations remain excluded in strict mode.
- Added `/panier`: manual selection of up to 100 fresh profile-eligible candidates, conditional 10% basket estimate only for recently observed Panier Malin eligibility, and optional user-confirmed delivery input. No purchase or automatic coupon stacking. Missing campaign eligibility never earns a discount. Newly discovered campaign data arrives through normal collection.
- Cards explicitly say prices exclude delivery. Product pages provide a delivery calculator and show extracted shipping text only as an observation requiring address confirmation. Automatic ranking by address-specific delivered cost is not implemented; we have no verified address-specific quote.
- Crawler discovers bounded same-origin campaign routes from the homepage each run, stripping tracking and excluding accounts/checkout. Newly discovered routes are logged with homepage provenance, included in that run, and fresh campaign listings become visible in weekly feeds. Discovery failures produce partial coverage; access refusal stops the run.
- Product parsing preserves colour × size × price combinations supplied by configurable-product data; stock stays unknown unless separately proved. Product pages show this explicitly. Different variant prices do not become a firm parent-product quote.
- Product pages offer conservative candidate comparisons for the same model and brand, including Anker/Soundcore aliases. No automatic merge or unverified savings claim. Candidates require a model reference and fresh observations.
- Source gender discrepancies get a warning. Official-looking seller prefixes no longer receive the known-alias bonus; exact aliases only. This does not independently authenticate a seller identity.
- Daily median baseline over up to 30 days needs five distinct prior days; repeated same-day polling cannot create confidence. Rebounds from temporary spikes no longer independently justify high deal ratings. Product pages display baseline availability.
